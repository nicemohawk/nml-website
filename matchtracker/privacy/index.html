MatchTracker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Nice Mohawk Limited

MatchTracker records soccer matches with your Apple Watch and shows them on your iPhone. Your matches stay on your own devices unless you choose to share them with a team. Outlines of the pitches you use are shared unless you turn that off. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell your data.

What stays on your devices

MatchTracker asks before it uses any of these, and you can change your mind in the Settings app or the Health app.

  • Health. It reads your soccer workouts, routes, distance, energy and heart rate. It saves the matches you record, and your effort rating if you give one, and removes matches you delete.
  • Location. During a match your watch records your route, so you can see where you played. On your iPhone, location centers the map and helps find fields nearby.
  • Motion. During a match, how hard your wrist is moving helps tell playing from resting.
  • Calendar. It reads the calendar you choose to find your next match. It never changes your calendar, and nothing in it leaves your device.
  • Photos, camera and microphone. Match video you import or record is processed on your device. Clips and pictures you make are saved to your photo library.

Maps and satellite pictures come from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

What you share with a team

Nothing is shared with a team until you join or start one. From then on, each match you play while sharing is on is sent to the MatchTracker server under that team's code. You can share with nobody, change teams or leave at any time in the Team tab. Matches from before you joined aren't sent.

Live heart rate isn't sent. Anyone with a team's code can join it and see what the team sees, so share codes only with people you play with. Teammates may keep copies of what they've seen.

Community fields

So that other players don't have to draw the same pitch, MatchTracker shares the outlines and names of the fields you use, and fields it finds in satellite pictures near you, along with your iPhone's random identifier. This is on when you install the app. Turn off Share Fields under Privacy in the app's Settings to stop.

Health information

Information from Apple Health is used only to show and analyze your matches. We don't use it for advertising or marketing, don't sell it, and don't give it to data brokers. It leaves your devices only as described above, when you share a match with a team, or when you export or share something yourself. MatchTracker isn't a medical device and nothing in it is medical advice.

Things you send yourself

When you share a picture, video, export or diagnostics file, it goes where you send it. Exports with a route include exactly where you played. A diagnostics file contains detailed match data, including locations and heart rate, so send one only to someone you trust to look into a problem.

Purchases

Apple handles payment, and we never receive your card details. If you subscribe, the App Store's proof of purchase is sent to our server so it can unlock what you paid for.

Who else handles data

Companies that host our server and database process data for us, and only to provide that service. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If MatchTracker changes hands, data may pass to the new operator under this policy, and we'll say so first. Our servers may be in the United States.

Keeping and deleting

  • On your devices: delete any match in the app. Deleting the app removes its own data. Workouts stay in Health until you remove them there.
  • On our server: shared data is kept until you delete it. In the app, go to Settings, Privacy, then Delete My Server Data. That removes your matches, live updates, team membership and identifier.
  • Field outlines that have been merged with other players' stay, without your identifier. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire.
  • Leaving a team stops new sharing. What you shared before stays with the team until you delete your server data.

Children

A parent or guardian should set up MatchTracker for a child under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live), decide whether to share with a team, and record their consent under Privacy in the app's Settings. Initials or a nickname are enough for a name. If you think a child has shared information without permission, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can use MatchTracker fully without joining a team. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to its use, and to complain to a regulator. Email us and we'll help. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Security

Data is encrypted on its way to our server, and your Apple devices protect what's stored on them. No service is perfectly secure, so think before sharing what you'd want kept private.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change affects what's shared, the app will say so too.
